Historical, gridded reanalysis estimates from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API using its default Best Match dataset. Best Match combines available IFS, ERA5, and ERA5-Land data, so the underlying model and grid resolution can vary by date and location. These are regional estimates, not measurements from a local gauge or weather station. Rainfall is daily rain accumulation in millimeters; temperature is daily mean at 2 m in °C.
